Privacy Policy

JP Auto-Apply — last updated September 11, 2026

Data we handle

JP Auto-Apply is a personal job-application assistant. In the course of using it, the following data is collected and stored:

  • Job details — job postings, titles, companies, and descriptions you choose to save or apply to.
  • Resume / documents — files you upload to include with your job applications.
  • Application emails — the cover-letter emails you generate and send to recruiters, including the recipient address and the content you approve.
  • Basic account details — the username and password hash used to sign in to your personal dashboard.

How your data is used

All data is used solely to operate the service for you: to display your job pipeline on the dashboard, to generate cover letters, and to send applications on your behalf to the recruiters you choose. Your data is never sold, rented, or shared with third parties for marketing purposes.

Data retention

Captured jobs and related content are automatically and permanently erased from the service 5 days after capture. Uploaded documents are stored only while they are used for your applications. You can delete any stored job or uploaded resume at any time from the dashboard.

Data security

Communication with the service is encrypted (HTTPS). Dashboard access is protected by a password. Emails are sent using OAuth-protected credentials owned by you.

Your choices

You may delete individual jobs and uploaded resumes at any time from the dashboard. If you want your data fully removed, contact us and the data will be deleted within 30 days.
